Acid Pickling & Brightening

ACITEK DRY ACID SALT
  • A dry acid salt used for making acid dips for removal of silicate and oxide films on ferrous and non-ferrous metals to ensure better adhesion of subsequent electrodeposits.
  • Replaces hazardous hydrofluoric acid.
  • Added to the acid dip for removal of silicate film.
  • Also used as an additive to pickling baths for removal of smut from aluminium alloys, stainless steel and titanium.
ACID INHIBITOR SERIES
  • An excellent inhibitor specially developed for hydrochloric and sulphuric acid pickling baths to reduce base metal attack during pickling.
FUME REDUCER
  • Suppresses fume during pickling.
  • Aids in uniform pickling action.
  • Emulsifies the oil carryover from alkaline soak cleaner.
KEMBRITE BC 20
  • Brightening dip for brass and copper components.
  • Brightens the component to produce a glossy finish.
  • A dip in 5% sulphuric acid is used after BC 20 to get the final finish.
