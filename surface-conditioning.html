Surface Conditioning

Powerful cleaners for ferrous and non-ferrous metals, enhancing cleaning efficiency

01

Alkaline Soak Cleaners

SURFOLIN SERIES
  • High efficiency cleaner suitable for ferrous and nonferrous metals
  • The cleaner has long service life with very good cleaning effect
  • Removes grease, soil, weak scale layers and leaves the surface active and bright
02

Alkaline Electrolytic Cleaners

SURFOLIN EL SERIES
  • Heavy duty chelated alkaline ANODIC cleaner for ferrous metals. Can be used as a cathodic cleaner for activating passive nickel.
SURFOLIN BC PROCESS
  • Good cathodic cleaner for copper and brass. Can also be effectively used as an anodic cleaner for copper and brass
03

Electrolytic Alkaline Derusting

SURFOLIN NC SERIES
  • An electrolytic scale and rust remover
  • Very good substitute for anodic Etches
  • Very good cleaner prior to electroless Nickel on steel
  • Non cyanide cleaner
04

Spray Cleaner

SURFOLIN SP PROCESS
  • Heavy duty chelated alkaline cleaner for ferrous metals
05

ACID PICKLING & BRIGHTENING

ACITEK DRY ACID SALT
  • A dry acid salt used for making acid dips for removal of silicate and oxide films ON ferrous and non ferrous metals to get better adhesion of subsequent electrodeposits.
  • Replaces hazardous Hydrofluoric acid.
  • Added to the acid dip forremoval of silicate film.
  • Also used as an additive to pickling baths for removal of smut from aluminium alloys, stainless Steel and titanium.
ACID INHIBITOR SERIES
  • An excellent inhibitor specially developed for Hydrochloric acid, Sulphuric acid pickling bath to reduce the base metal attack during pickling.
FUME REDUCER
  • Suppresses fume during pickling.
  • Aids in uniform pickling action.
  • Emulsifies the oil carryover from carryover from alkaline soak cleaner.
KEMBRITE BC 20
  • Brightening dip for brass & copper components.
  • Brighten the component to produce a glossy finish.
  • A dip in 5% Sulphuric acid is used after BC 20 to get finish.
