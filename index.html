Why Choose US

Established in 1989, Artek Surfin Chemicals Ltd. is a trusted name in surface finishing, offering high-performance chemistries for metal finishing, anodising, electro-colouring, precious plating, and PCB applications. With ISO-certified plants and strong R&D, we deliver scalable, eco-friendly solutions across India and global markets. A 300+ member team drives our ₹3000+ crore success through innovation, reliability, and industry-specific expertise.
